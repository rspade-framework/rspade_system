QUILL 2 CHECKLISTS SURVIVE safe_html(); THE TOOLBAR BUTTON AND THE READ VIEW ARE A TEMPLATE PORT
Date: 2026-09-24

IF YOU DO NOTHING: your rich-text editor offers no checklist button, and a
    checklist that reaches a field anyway (pasted, or from another client)
    keeps its ticks through save but shows in your read view (your copy of
    Rich_Text_Display) as plain bullets, so a reader cannot see which items
    are ticked.

SUMMARY
    Quill 2 stores a checklist item's state as li[data-list="checked"] or
    li[data-list="unchecked"]. Both safe_html() implementations now keep that
    attribute - on an li only, and only with Quill's four values (checked,
    unchecked, ordered, bullet). Before, it was stripped, and every checklist
    became a plain list on save. Nothing else in the allow-list changed.

    Contract: php artisan rsx:man safe_html (CHECKLISTS)

THE REFERENCE IMPLEMENTATION
    system/app/RSpade/resource/reference_app/theme/components/inputs/wysiwyg/
    wysiwyg_input.js
        { 'list': 'check' } in the toolbar's list group, beside ordered and
        bullet.
    system/app/RSpade/resource/reference_app/theme/components/view/
    rich_text_display/rich_text_display.scss
        The block commented "A checklist item": li[data-list="checked"] and
        li[data-list="unchecked"] lose their list marker and gain a drawn box
        (::before) and, when checked, a tick (::after), in theme tokens.

PORTING IT
    Copy that block into your Rich_Text_Display stylesheet (or whatever
    component prints your rich-text type), and add { 'list': 'check' } to the
    list group of your Wysiwyg_Input toolbar, beside ordered and bullet - the
    reference app's theme/components/inputs/wysiwyg/wysiwyg_input.js is the
    worked example. Bullet and checklist stay distinct formats: a bullet item
    stores as a plain li, a checklist item as li[data-list].

VERIFICATION
    Author a checklist, tick an item, save, reload the edit page: the tick is
    still there. On the read view the ticked item shows a ticked box.
