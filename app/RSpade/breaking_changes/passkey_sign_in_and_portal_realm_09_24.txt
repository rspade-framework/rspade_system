PASSWORDLESS PASSKEY SIGN-IN, AND PASSKEYS / 2FA / SSO FOR THE CLIENT PORTAL - THE ENGINE SHIPS, THE SCREENS DO NOT
Date: 2026-09-24

IF YOU DO NOTHING: nothing breaks and nothing changes - your staff login page
    offers no "Sign in with a passkey", your portal users still sign in with a
    password alone (no second factor, no passkey, no Google/Microsoft), and
    the new framework surfaces stay dormant until you port the screens
    described below.

SUMMARY
    Three capabilities arrived with the pull:

    1. PASSWORDLESS SIGN-IN. A passkey can now be the FIRST and only
       credential: Rsx_Two_Factor::begin_passkey_login() /
       verify_passkey_login(), driven by the new <Passkey_Sign_In> component.
       User verification is required, and a passwordless sign-in is COMPLETE -
       it owes no second factor (framework ruling; rsx:man two_factor,
       PASSWORDLESS SIGN-IN). Every passkey your users already registered can
       do this; nothing needs re-enrolling.

    2. THE PORTAL REALM FOR SECOND FACTORS AND PASSKEYS. Rsx_Portal_Two_Factor
       is the identical API for Portal_User_Model - TOTP, passkeys, recovery
       codes, the challenge and passwordless sign-in - in its own table
       (_portal_two_factor_credentials, created by the framework migration).

    3. THE PORTAL REALM FOR FEDERATED SIGN-IN. Rsx_Portal_Sso is the
       identical API to Rsx_Sso for portal users, with its own table, its own
       ceremony URLs and its own portal.sso.* hooks. It is OFF until you set
       rsx.sso.portal_enabled.

    The staff facades kept their names and signatures; nothing you wrote
    against Rsx_Two_Factor or Rsx_Sso changes.

    Full contract: php artisan rsx:man two_factor, rsx:man sso, rsx:man portal
    Recipes: skills rspade:two-factor, rspade:sso, rspade:portal-core

    Nothing to do in this section - it is all in system/ and it is all live.

    The shipped components (<Two_Factor_Challenge>, <Passkey_Register>,
    <Totp_Enrollment>, <Sso_Buttons>, <Passkey_Sign_In>) pick their realm from
    the page, so they work on a portal page with no argument.

WHAT IS MISSING IN YOUR APPLICATION
    The interface in /rsx/, without which nobody can reach the new
    capabilities:

    - Staff cannot sign in with a passkey: the login page has no button and
      there is no endpoint for it to post to.
    - Portal users cannot enroll a passkey or an authenticator app: the
      portal settings screen has no section for it.
    - A portal user who did enroll would not be challenged: the portal login
      signs in straight after the password, and there is no verify page.
    - Portal users cannot sign in with Google or Microsoft: the switch is
      off, no portal.sso.* policy handler exists, and the login page shows no
      buttons.

THE REFERENCE IMPLEMENTATION
    Every file below is release-current under
    system/app/RSpade/resource/reference_app/:

    app/login/login_controller.php
        passkey_login() - the staff passwordless endpoint: verify_passkey_login()
        then post_login_destination().
    app/login/login_index.blade.php, login_index.scss
        One .login-alternatives block: the "or" divider, then
        <Passkey_Sign_In $controller="Login_Controller" $method="passkey_login" />,
        then the SSO buttons - hidden as a whole when nothing in it is visible
        (a browser without passkeys marks the control --unsupported).

    portal/auth/Portal_Login_Controller.php
        index() is TWO-STAGE: after check_password() + can_login(), a portal
        user with Rsx_Portal_Two_Factor::is_enabled() is parked
        (begin_challenge()) and redirected to verify() instead of being signed
        in; the invited-client id rides Portal_Session::put_value(). verify()
        (/login/verify), verify_2fa() and passkey_login() are new;
        post_auth_destination() is now public and raises the welcome flash.
    portal/auth/portal_login_verify.blade.php
        <Two_Factor_Challenge $controller="Portal_Login_Controller"
        $method="verify_2fa" />.
    portal/auth/portal_login_index.blade.php, portal_login_index.scss
        The divider, <Passkey_Sign_In ...passkey_login />, and <Sso_Buttons />
        behind Rsx_Portal_Sso::is_enabled().
    handlers/Portal_Sso_Handlers.php
        The five portal.sso.* hooks: a verified provider email matching a
        portal user of the declared site is connected and signed in; anything
        else declines.
    portal/settings/Portal_Settings_Security.jqhtml|.js|.scss, and one line in
    portal/settings/Portal_Settings_Action.jqhtml
        Passkeys and authenticator app (list, remove, <Passkey_Register />, an
        on-demand <Totp_Enrollment />, recovery codes) and Connected Accounts
        (list, disconnect, <Sso_Buttons $intent="link" />).

PORTING IT
    Copy the pieces you want - they are independent:

    - Staff passwordless only: the login_controller.php method and the three
      lines of login_index.blade.php. Adjust the destination call to yours.
    - Portal second factors: the Portal_Login_Controller changes, the verify
      blade and the settings region. If your portal login controller has
      diverged, port the BRANCH, not the file: the is_enabled() test sits
      between can_login() and set_portal_user_id().
    - Portal SSO: set 'sso' => ['portal_enabled' => true] in
      rsx/resource/config/rsx.php, copy Portal_Sso_Handlers.php (rewrite
      match_verified_email() to your onboarding policy - an invite-based
      portal may resolve an open invitation there instead), and REGISTER THE
      PORTAL CALLBACK in each provider's console beside the staff one -
      https://<APP_URL host>/_portal/_sso/<key>/callback in prefix mode, or
      https://<portal domain>/_sso/<key>/callback with rsx.portal.domain
      (Rsx_Portal_Sso::callback_url('<key>') returns the exact string).

    A portal that already has a second factor of its own (a bespoke TOTP)
    migrates its users by removing the bespoke enrollment and asking them to
    enroll through Portal_Settings_Security; the framework cannot read another
    implementation's seeds.
