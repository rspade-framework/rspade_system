VIEW AS CLIENT NO LONGER MARKS THE CLIENT ACTIVE
Date: 2026-10-31

IF YOU DO NOTHING: your copy of rsx/portal_main.php keeps stamping clients.portal_last_activity_at on every request a staff member makes while viewing the portal as a client, so a client's "Portal Activity" reads as active whenever somebody on your staff looked at their portal.

SUMMARY
    Category 1: a /rsx/ template change.

    View as Client is read-only. The framework refuses every unmarked portal Ajax
    endpoint while the portal session is impersonated, and now refuses a portal
    upload too (nothing to do for that - it arrived in system/). The template's
    per-request activity stamp is application code the framework cannot see, so
    the template now skips it while impersonating.

AFFECTED FILES
    /rsx/portal_main.php

CHANGES REQUIRED

    File: /rsx/portal_main.php
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    In pre_dispatch(), replace

        static::__stamp_portal_activity();

    with

        if (!Portal_Session::is_impersonating()) {
            static::__stamp_portal_activity();
        }

    (Portal_Session is already imported there.) Do the same for any other write
    your Portal_Main::pre_dispatch() makes on every request.

VERIFICATION
    Note a client's portal_last_activity_at, begin View as Client on one of its
    contacts from the staff app, browse the portal, and read the column again: it
    has not moved. Sign in to the portal as that contact yourself and it moves.
