FORM SEEDING REMOVED: Rsx_Formdata_Generator_Controller AND Rsx_Form.seed() ARE GONE
Date: 2026-10-28

IF YOU DO NOTHING: any page of yours that still names Rsx_Formdata_Generator_Controller - the template's client edit page passes Rsx.Route('Rsx_Formdata_Generator_Controller::...') as a $seeder on its fields - throws when it renders, because the route no longer exists.

SUMMARY
    Category 2. Form seeding - filling a form with random test values from a
    debug-mode button - is removed from the framework and the template.

    The button itself was removed with the old form component when the form
    engine moved into framework core; what remained could not be reached:
    Rsx_Form.seed(), a seed() method on the template's field base and several
    inputs, $seeder arguments on the client and contact edit pages, and the
    Rsx_Formdata_Generator_Controller Ajax endpoints those arguments pointed at
    (random names, cities, phone numbers and words from bundled word lists).
    The controller was a live web surface that nothing used.

    Realistic test data belongs in the database, made by a task: the
    template's Seeder_Service (seed_clients, seed_contacts, seed_all) is the
    worked example.

THE CHANGE
    - Deleted: system/app/RSpade/Core/Testing/Rsx_Formdata_Generator_Controller.php
      and its word lists (Core/Testing/resource/).
    - Deleted: Rsx_Form.seed().
    - Template: the seed() methods of Form_Field_Abstract, Text_Input,
      Select_Input, Checkbox_Input, Profile_Photo_Input, Wysiwyg_Input and
      Raw_Text_Input, the $seeder argument documentation in their templates,
      and every $seeder argument on Clients_Edit_Action and
      Contacts_Edit_Action.

ACTION REQUIRED
    1. Remove every $seeder argument from your templates. A Route() to the
       controller throws; a plain string is inert and only noise:

           grep -rn 'seeder' rsx/ --include=*.jqhtml

    2. Remove any seed() method from your form fields and inputs, and any call
       to a form's or a component's seed():

           grep -rn 'seed()' rsx/

    3. If your application relied on filling forms with test data, seed the
       database instead - a #[Task] in the shape of
       system/app/RSpade/resource/reference_app/services/seeder_service.php.

VERIFICATION
    - grep -rn 'Rsx_Formdata_Generator_Controller\|seeder=' rsx/ finds nothing.
    - Your client and contact add/edit pages render with no console error
      (php artisan rsx:debug /clients/add --user=1).
