A SHARE DRIVES THE INVITATION: SHARE LINKS, A SEPARATE SEND STEP, AND LOGIN NOTICES
Date: 2026-10-31

IF YOU DO NOTHING: your portal keeps its own share emails and sign-in pages and nothing breaks, but Login_Notice has no notices to show, a redirect threaded through your accept-invite or forgot-password pages may still be dropped, and an expired invitation still offers your visitors an account if you kept the template's allow_expired page.

SUMMARY
    Category 3: a requested feature whose implementation includes a template UI.

    Portal access is by invitation, and a document share is how most clients first
    meet the portal. The template now lets the share drive the invitation: a share
    mints it, sending is its own step, and the share link leads each follower to
    the right place and back to the document.

WHAT YOU ALREADY HAVE
    Arrived in system/, nothing to do:

    - Login_Notice: params($code) and current(), and the rsx.login_notices config
      key (empty until your application declares codes).
    - Passkey_Sign_In sends the threaded redirect with its call.

WHAT IS MISSING IN YOUR APPLICATION
    Everything below is template code, so it arrives in your /rsx/ only if you port it:

    - /shared/:token: the link a recipient follows, signed out or in.
    - The staff send step: the email as it will be read, each recipient's link
      with a Copy control, Close or Send.
    - Expired, used, revoked and unknown invitations going to the sign-in page with
      an error, in place of the expired-invitation account page.
    - The redirect carried through accept-invite, account-exists, forgot password
      and the second factor, and beating the invited client's workspace.

    portal/auth/Portal_Password_Reset_Controller.php  and its blades: the redirect
    portal/auth/portal_login_index.blade.php      the notice block

PORTING IT
    Declare your notices first: the sign-in pages and every flow that sends a
    visitor to them read the same codes. Replace any ?message= handling with
    Login_Notice::params() and the notice block.

    A share link is public and opens nothing by itself; keep the document's own
    access rules where they are. Mark an invitation minted by a share as still owing
    its email, so one your staff already sent is not sent twice. Where the screens go
    is yours: your navigation, permissions and design system decide.

VERIFICATION
    Share a document with a contact who has no account: one invitation, no email.
    Send: two emails. Follow the link signed out, accept, set a password: the
    document's page opens. Expire the invitation and follow the link again: the
    sign-in page with the invitation error. As an account holder, signed out: the
    sign-in page with the notice, then, through a second factor, the document.
